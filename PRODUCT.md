# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Quote readers, contributors, and curators discover, share, and organize attributed quotations from books, films, music, games, speeches, and other sources.
- Administrators and moderators maintain the catalogue, review submissions, and prepare editorial themes. The primary admin workflow is content moderation and theme curation.

## Product Purpose

Verbatims is a quote platform for discovering and sharing memorable, attributed quotations. Success means that readers can find and collect useful quotes while contributors and moderators can keep the catalogue accurate and curated.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The application supports quote publishing and moderation, author/reference records, lists, favourites, themes, imports, exports, and operational maintenance.
- Admin redesigns should preserve existing operations by default. A feature should only be removed after its purpose and impact have been reviewed and explained.
- Theme creation should favor curated, reproducible inputs and validated tooling over automatic AI-generated theme suggestions.
- The web stack is Nuxt 4, Vue 3, Cloudflare Workers, D1/SQLite, Drizzle ORM, UnaUI, and UnoCSS.

## Brand Commitments

- The product is refined, premium, timeless, and editorial; typography and whitespace carry hierarchy, with restrained color and purposeful motion.
- The interface must support both light and dark themes.
