# When night speaks — Verbatims

An editorial home for memorable lines.

For this Halloween edition, Verbatims follows the literature that lingers after dark. A moonlit cemetery sets the scene as Shakespeare’s warning from *Macbeth* rises over the trees in pale serif type. Below, monochrome portraits and illustrated book covers lead deeper into the stories that haunt us.
