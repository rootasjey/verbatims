Mary Shelley

The mind behind a creature that outlived its century.

English novelist · 1797—1851

She gave the unknown a voice.

Author of Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus

Daughter of Mary Wollstonecraft and William Godwin, Shelley grew up among radical ideas. A ghost-story challenge at Lake Geneva in 1816 became Frankenstein, begun when she was eighteen and published anonymously two years later.

It was on a dreary night of November that I beheld the accomplishment of my toils.
